# Marketing Performance Readout

A skill that turns campaign or channel metrics into a readout with the
standards of a marketing analyst and a channel manager, in one pass:
results against targets, and only the claims the data supports.

## What you get

- Results against each target, with the gap and a verdict.
- What made up most of the result, with timing kept apart from cause.
- What to stop, keep and start, each tied to a number or labeled a test.

## Configuring

Paste the metrics exports and the targets, or the campaign brief that
set them. Spend, a prior period and notes on changes made mid-run make
the readout sharper. It never changes budgets or campaigns.
