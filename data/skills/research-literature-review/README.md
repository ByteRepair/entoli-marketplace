# Research Literature Review

A skill that turns a research question and a set of papers into a
structured review, in one pass, with every claim tied to the paper it
comes from.

## What you get

- The themes in the set, what is settled, what conflicts (with the
  papers on each side) and what is still open.
- A reference list copied from DBLP, or from arXiv or the publisher when
  DBLP has no entry, with anything it could not check marked
  "unverified".
- A list of papers it could not read, none of which is cited.

## Configuring

State the question and paste the papers or give links, DOIs or arXiv
IDs. A scope (years, venues, methods), a length limit (the default:
about 1,500 words) and a citation style make the review fit its use.
