# Marketing Campaign Brief

A skill that turns a goal, an audience and a budget into one campaign
brief, with the standards of a campaign manager and a media planner, in
one pass.

## What you get

- One message in one sentence, with the facts that back it.
- One success metric with a target and a date.
- Channels with their role and a budget split that adds up to the total.
- The offer, a dated timeline, and what the campaign will not do.

## Configuring

Give the goal, the audience, the budget and the window. Past results by
channel let it set targets from a baseline instead of marking them
"needs baseline"; brand rules and product facts keep the message
grounded.
