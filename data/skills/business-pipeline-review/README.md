# Business Pipeline Review

A skill that prepares the weekly pipeline review with the standards of a
sales manager and a revenue operations analyst, in one pass, from your CRM
export.

## What you get

- The coverage table per rep and in total, with a risk-adjusted line.
- Five to eight stalled-deal callouts, each with the question that would
  unblock it.
- A forecast draft by band (commit, best case, pipeline) with one line of
  reason per deal, and one coaching question per at-risk deal.
- The export's gaps, named instead of averaged over.

## Configuring

Paste the open-deals export (stage, amount, close date, last activity,
next step, owner) and the quarter target, and give the review date. Stage
norms and per-rep quotas make the review sharper.
