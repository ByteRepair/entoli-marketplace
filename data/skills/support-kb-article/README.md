# Support KB Article

A skill that turns resolved tickets or an engineer's notes into a
help-center article with the standards of a support agent and a
technical writer, in one pass.

## What you get

- One article with a title and opening in the words customers search
  for, and the exact error text quoted.
- The cause in plain words, numbered steps with screenshot
  placeholders, and what to do if it still fails.
- Notes for the reviewer: labels to confirm and gaps in the fix.

## Configuring

Paste the resolved tickets or the engineer's notes on the problem and
its fix. Your style guide, UI labels and related articles make it
sharper. Nothing is published.
