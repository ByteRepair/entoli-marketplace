# Finance Month-End Close

A skill that runs the month-end close with the standards of a close
coordinator and an FP&A analyst, in one pass.

## What you get

- The close tracker (step, owner, status, blocker) and the outstanding
  list in dependency order, with close-day targets.
- A variance table against budget or prior period, material lines only,
  with the rest summed.
- A flux memo, one paragraph per material variance, each driver
  evidenced or marked unexplained, and what to check before sign-off.

## Configuring

Paste the close checklist and the status of each step, the period and
the P&L export. The budget, your materiality threshold and the events
you know about make the memo sharper. Nothing is posted, locked or
signed off.
