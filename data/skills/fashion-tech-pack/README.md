# Fashion Tech Pack

A skill that writes, reviews and revises a style's tech pack with the
standards of a technical designer and a fit-session scribe, in one pass,
so a factory can sew from it without asking questions.

## What you get

- Pack sections in factory order, with a tolerance on every point of
  measure and a BOM that matches the construction notes.
- Pack reviews that list every gap, with the field and the fix.
- From a fit session: the fit-notes grid, factory instructions, and
  revision-log entries in one format, with contradicting comments kept
  for the fitter.

## Configuring

Give the style and the round it serves, then the design and
measurements, the pack to review, or the fit session's raw comments and
measurement deltas. It keeps your imperial or metric units as you
started.
