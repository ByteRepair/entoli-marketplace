# Marketing Content Calendar

A skill that plans a month or a quarter of content with the standards of
a content strategist and a managing editor, in one pass: every piece
tied to a goal, and the plan kept inside what the team can make.

## What you get

- A dated calendar: channel, format, working title, owner, goal and
  draft-due date for every slot.
- The load per owner per week, checked against capacity.
- The pieces cut to fit, and the goals left without content.

## Configuring

Give the period, the themes or goals, the channels and the team's
capacity. Add fixed dates, owners and past results by format to sharpen
the plan. It never schedules or publishes anything.
