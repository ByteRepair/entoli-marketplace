# Support Escalation

A skill that turns a support ticket thread into an engineering
escalation with the standards of a senior support engineer, in one
pass.

## What you get

- A title, a severity with its reason, and a one-sentence summary.
- Repro steps, environment, quoted error text, impact and what support
  already tried.
- The precise ask, and every unknown listed as a question with who can
  answer it.

## Configuring

Paste the ticket thread. Logs, account details, the product version
and your escalation template make it sharper. Nothing is filed and the
customer is not contacted.
