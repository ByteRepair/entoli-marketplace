# Support Weekly Trends

A skill that writes the weekly support report from a ticket export with
the standards of a support operations analyst and a support team lead,
in one pass.

## What you get

- Volume for the week: created, solved and open, by channel and
  category.
- Top themes with definitions, counts, share and example tickets.
- What changed against last week, with causes only where the material
  supports them.
- Product and help-center fixes ranked by the tickets they would cut.

## Configuring

Paste this week's ticket export, and last week's to compare. Response
times, satisfaction scores, and the week's incidents and releases make
the report sharper. No ticket is changed.
