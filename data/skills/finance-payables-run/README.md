# Finance Payables Run

A skill that prepares a payment run with the standards of an accounts
payable clerk, in one pass.

## What you get

- An exception report: duplicates, missing approvals, PO mismatches,
  and new or changed bank details flagged for verification, each with
  the check to run.
- Coding suggestions against your chart of accounts.
- The payment-run memo (total, per-vendor list, terms, holds with
  reasons) and short vendor follow-up drafts.

## Configuring

Paste the bills export, the payment run date and your approval rules.
PO and receipt records, invoices and your chart of accounts make the
checks complete. Nothing is paid or scheduled, and bank details are
never changed.
