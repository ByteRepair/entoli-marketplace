# Job Resume Tailor

A skill that tailors your resume to one job posting with the standards
of a resume writer and a recruiter, in one pass.

## What you get

- Your resume reordered and reworded to the posting's terms, using only
  your real experience and the numbers you gave.
- A change list showing what moved, what was reworded and what was cut.
- The gaps it could not close, and the places where a number would help.

## Configuring

Paste your resume and the job posting. Run Job Fit Check first in the
same conversation and this skill builds on its report. The skill never
sends or submits your resume.
