# People Survey Readout

A skill that turns employee survey results into a readout with the
standards of a people analyst and an HR business partner, in one pass,
while keeping the people who answered anonymous.

## What you get

- The numbers that moved since the last survey, and the response rate.
- Themes from the comments with how many comments each rests on, with
  no quote that could identify its writer.
- Two to four proposed actions, each tied to a theme or a score, and
  the limits of the data.

## Configuring

Paste the scores by question with the number of answers, the response
rate and the open comments. The last survey's scores and group
breakdowns show what changed; groups under five answers are not shown.
