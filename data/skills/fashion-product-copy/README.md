# Fashion Product Copy

A skill that writes a drop's customer-facing copy with the standards of
an e-commerce writer, in one pass: product pages first, then the email
and captions from them, so the drop reads the same on every channel.

## What you get

- Product page copy: one opening paragraph and a detail list.
- Size-guide text that states only what your data supports.
- The drop email: subject under 60 characters, body under 150 words,
  one call to action.
- Two to four captions per style per platform, under 200 characters.

## Configuring

Give the style facts (fabric, fit, care, colorways, price), or paste
your line-sheet copy, plus a voice sample and the drop's date and
channels. It never publishes or schedules anything.
