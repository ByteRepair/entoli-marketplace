# People Performance Review

A skill that drafts a manager's review of one employee with the standards
of a people manager and an HR business partner, in one pass. Ratings are
proposals; the manager decides.

## What you get

- Each goal with its evidence and a proposed rating, or "not enough
  evidence" where the notes do not support one.
- Strengths and growth areas tied to evidence, each growth area with a
  next step, and goals for the next period.
- Notes for the manager: fairness flags, evidence gaps and points to
  raise in the conversation.

## Configuring

Paste the manager's notes, the employee's goals and the rating scale.
Peer feedback and the self-review make the draft fairer.
