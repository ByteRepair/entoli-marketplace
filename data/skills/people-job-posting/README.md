# People Job Posting

A skill that writes a job description and a public job posting with the
standards of a recruiter and a hiring manager, in one pass, from the
hiring manager's notes. You post the role.

## What you get

- An internal job description: purpose, first-year outcomes, level and
  reporting line.
- A public posting under 500 words, with must-haves kept apart from
  nice-to-haves, in plain and inclusive wording.
- A salary range only when you give one, and a list of what is still
  missing.

## Configuring

Paste the hiring manager's notes with the title, the work, the team, the
location or remote policy and the employment type. The salary range, the
level and the hiring process make the posting complete.
