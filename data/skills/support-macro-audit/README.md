# Support Macro Audit

A skill that reviews a library of canned responses (macros) with the
standards of a support team lead and a support content editor, in one
pass.

## What you get

- A verdict for every macro: keep, rewrite, merge or retire, with the
  issue and the source.
- Outdated, duplicated and wrong-tone macros, each quoted.
- Proposed rewrites, and new drafts for top themes with no macro.

## Configuring

Paste your macros and the current policies and product notes. Your top
ticket themes, macro usage counts and tone guide make the audit
sharper. No macro is changed in your help desk.
