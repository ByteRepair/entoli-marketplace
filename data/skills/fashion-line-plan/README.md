# Fashion Line Plan

A skill that plans a seasonal collection with the standards of a
merchandiser and a planner, in one pass: categories sized against what
sold, price tiers that step up, and units checked against the budget
month by month.

## What you get

- The line plan: categories with style counts, price tier ranges and the
  newness against carryover split.
- Open-to-buy notes by month, with every month over budget flagged.
- A line-review memo: the architecture, the cut list with reasons, the
  add list with the holes they fill, and open questions.

## Configuring

Name the delivery season and give the price and margin targets, the
revenue budget and the carryover rule. Attach last season's
sell-through, or the plan is marked "unvalidated by history". A trend
brief, if you have one, sets the theme for the new styles.
