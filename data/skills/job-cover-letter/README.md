# Job Cover Letter

A skill that writes a cover letter for one job with the standards of a
career coach and a hiring manager, in one pass.

## What you get

- A letter under one page that leads with the two or three things that
  matter most for this role.
- Company details only from what you gave, and claims only your resume
  supports.
- Notes on why it leads where it does and what would make it stronger.

## Configuring

Paste the job posting, your resume and why you want the role. What you
know about the company and a sample of your writing make the letter
sound like you. The skill never sends the letter.
