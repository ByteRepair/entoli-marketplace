# Job Application Tracker

A skill that keeps your job search in order. It keeps one tracker of
every application, with a stage, a next step and a date for each, and
lists what is due in the next week.

## What you get

- The tracker: company, role, stage, fit call, next step and date for
  every application.
- What is due in the next 7 days, overdue first: deadlines, follow-ups,
  interviews, offers to answer.
- Which job-seeking skill to run next for each step.

## Configuring

Share postings, applications sent, replies and interview dates as they
happen. The tracker is kept in `records/job-application-tracker.md` in
your project workspace. Put a weekly trigger on the skill for a
check-in.
