# Research Paper Check

A skill that reads your own draft the way a reviewer at your target venue
will, before you submit it, and returns a ranked list of fixes.

## What you get

- The contribution in two or three plain sentences, and where the
  abstract or introduction says something else.
- Claims against evidence, missing baselines, related work gaps with
  checked references, and clarity notes.
- Every finding ranked (blocks acceptance, should fix, nice to have)
  with a rough effort.

## Configuring

Paste the draft and name the venue. The call for papers, page limit,
review criteria and earlier reviews make the check match what reviewers
there will ask. It never rewrites or submits the paper.
