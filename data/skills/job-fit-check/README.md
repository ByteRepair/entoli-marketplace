# Job Fit Check

A skill that checks how well you fit one job with the standards of a
recruiter and a career coach, in one pass.

## What you get

- Each requirement in the posting sorted as met, partly met or missing,
  with the resume line that shows it.
- An honest fit call (strong fit, worth applying, stretch or poor fit)
  that never inflates your fit.
- What to stress on your application, and what to learn or show for the
  gaps.

## Configuring

Paste the job posting and your resume. Experience not on your resume and
what you want from the job make the call more useful. The skill never
applies for you.
