# Job Offer Negotiation

A skill that plans how to negotiate a job offer with the standards of a
career coach and a compensation adviser, in one pass.

## What you get

- The offer's full yearly value, with the arithmetic shown.
- What to ask for, in what order, each with a reason and a fallback.
- Your walk-away point and a draft reply, never sent for you.

## Configuring

Paste the offer and your priorities. Market data you have, other offers
and your current pay make the asks stronger. The skill uses no salary
figures you did not give.
