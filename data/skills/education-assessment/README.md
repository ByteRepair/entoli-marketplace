# Education Assessment

A skill that builds a quiz, test or exam with the standards of a subject
teacher and an exam moderator, in one pass.

## What you get

- A paper ready to print, with every question mapped to an outcome and
  answerable from the material covered.
- An answer key and a marking rubric two markers would apply the same
  way.
- A blueprint showing marks per outcome and the spread of difficulty.

## Configuring

Give the outcomes to assess, the material covered, the level and the
time allowed. Total marks, question types and past papers make the paper
match what your students expect.
