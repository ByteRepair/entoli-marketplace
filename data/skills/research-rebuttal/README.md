# Research Rebuttal

A skill that drafts your response to reviewers, in one pass: every point
answered, grouped by theme, specific about what will change, and within
the venue's length limit.

## What you get

- The response in the venue's format, each answer tagged with the
  reviewer points it covers.
- A coverage table showing where every point is answered, and the list
  of planned revision changes.
- Items it had to guess marked "[authors confirm]", and the length
  against the limit.

## Configuring

Paste the reviews, the paper and the venue's rebuttal rules (length
limit, format, whether new results are allowed). Your position on each
point and any new results keep the draft from guessing. It never
submits the response or invents results.
