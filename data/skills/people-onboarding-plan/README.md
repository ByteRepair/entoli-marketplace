# People Onboarding Plan

A skill that writes a new hire's first 90 days with the standards of a
hiring manager, a people operations partner and an IT administrator, in
one pass.

## What you get

- A before-day-one checklist: every account, tool and piece of equipment
  with an owner and a ready-by date.
- The first week day by day, with who runs each part.
- Two to four goals each for 30, 60 and 90 days, and the checkpoints
  with the questions to ask.

## Configuring

Give the role and level, the start date, the manager, the team and the
tools the person will use. A buddy, the first project and the people to
meet make the plan complete.
