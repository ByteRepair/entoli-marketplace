# Finance Collections

A skill that runs the receivables follow-up with the standards of a
collections clerk, in one pass.

## What you get

- A ranked queue: customer, total past due, oldest invoice, rung, last
  contact, action.
- An expected pay date and confidence for each receivable, ready to
  feed the cash forecast.
- One dunning draft per customer at its rung, and a resolve-first list
  for disputed accounts.
- Suggestions for matching unapplied payments to invoices.

## Configuring

Paste the AR aging and your recent contact history. Your dunning
ladder, unapplied payments and your confidence per customer make it
sharper; the default ladder is the due date, then 15, 30, 60 and 90
days. Nothing is sent, applied, credited or written off.
