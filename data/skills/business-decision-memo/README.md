# Business Decision Memo

A skill that writes a one-page decision memo with the standards of a chief
of staff and a finance analyst, in one pass. It recommends; the decider
decides.

## What you get

- The question, the criteria, and the options compared on those criteria
  only, each marked reversible or one-way.
- Every fact with its basis: your data, a stated assumption, or unknown
  with a date.
- A one-sentence recommendation, the second choice, the key risks, and
  the observable fact that would reverse the call.

## Configuring

Give the question, the options, the constraints (budget, timing,
dependencies), the deadline and who decides. Known costs and benefits per
option make the comparison sharper.
