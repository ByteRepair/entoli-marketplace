# Finance Cash Forecast

A skill that builds a rolling 13-week cash forecast with the standards
of a cash manager, in one pass.

## What you get

- The weekly forecast: beginning cash, receipts, payments, ending cash,
  and your floor, with the opening balances stated.
- A low-cash case without the uncertain receipts.
- The bridge from last week's forecast, real changes only.
- Shortfall weeks with their causes and two or three proposed actions.

## Configuring

Paste your bank balances, the AP export, the AR aging and the fixed
payment dates, and say which week to start. The expected pay dates from
the finance-collections skill make the receipts sharper. No money is
moved and no credit is drawn.
