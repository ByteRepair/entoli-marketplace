# Support Ticket Replies

A skill that drafts replies to a batch of support tickets with the
standards of a support agent and a support team lead, in one pass.

## What you get

- One draft reply per ticket, ready to paste, in the customer's tone.
- Every answer tied to a help-center article or policy you supplied.
- No refund, credit or date the policy does not allow.
- Escalation flags with the role each ticket goes to and why.

## Configuring

Paste the tickets and the help-center articles and policies that
apply. A tone guide and account notes make the replies sharper. Nothing
is sent, refunded or closed.
