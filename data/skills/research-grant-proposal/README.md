# Research Grant Proposal

A skill that drafts a grant proposal from your aims and the funder's
call, with the standards of a principal investigator and a grants
officer, in one pass.

## What you get

- Aims, significance, approach, timeline and risks, in the call's
  section order and within its limits.
- A budget justification built only from your numbers, with the
  arithmetic shown and the call's caps checked.
- A map from each review criterion to the part of the draft that
  answers it.

## Configuring

Give your research aims, paste or link the funder's call, and give the
budget numbers. Preliminary results, the team and the funder's template
make the draft closer to final. It never adds aims, invents results or
submits the proposal.
