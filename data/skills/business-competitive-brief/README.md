# Business Competitive Brief

A skill that builds a one-page competitor battlecard with the standards of
a product manager and an account executive, in one pass, from the material
you paste.

## What you get

- A trade-off table: their pitch, your counter, and the source of each.
- Where you win and where you lose, each with the deal or ticket behind
  it, and unverified claims marked as such.
- Objection responses for what buyers actually said, watch-outs for the
  next release, and a research list for the card's weak spots.

## Configuring

Name the competitor and paste their material (pricing pages, docs,
release notes) with your win/loss and support notes. Your own pricing and
the buyer segment to write for make the card sharper.
