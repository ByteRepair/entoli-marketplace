# Education Lesson Plan

A skill that plans one lesson with the standards of a classroom teacher
and a learning support teacher, in one pass.

## What you get

- One clear objective that fits the time and what students already
  know.
- Timed activities that add up to the lesson length, with checks for
  understanding and their expected answers.
- Support and stretch for each main activity, and a materials list.

## Configuring

Give the topic, the level, the lesson length and what students already
know. The course outcome, class size, known needs and equipment make the
plan fit your class.
