# Job Interview Prep

A skill that prepares you for one job interview with the standards of an
interview coach and a hiring manager, in one pass.

## What you get

- The questions this interview is likely to ask, with the three most
  likely marked.
- A STAR answer outline for each, built only from your real stories.
- Honest answers for your gaps, and questions to ask the interviewers.

## Configuring

Paste the job posting, your resume and the interview type. Three to five
of your own work stories make the outlines yours. Run Job Fit Check
first and this skill builds on its gaps.
