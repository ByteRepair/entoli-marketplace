# Business Deal Follow-up

A skill that wraps up a sales call with the standards of an account
executive and a sales manager, in one pass, from your call notes. You send
the email and update the CRM.

## What you get

- A follow-up email under 150 words: their problem restated, dated next
  steps, one call to action.
- A deal summary with budget, authority, need and timeline each marked
  evidenced, hinted or missing, plus risks, champion status and a line
  for the CRM.
- Next steps with an owner and a date, or "to confirm".

## Configuring

Paste the call notes or transcript, name the prospect and the people on
the call, and say whether it was a first discovery call or a later
meeting.
