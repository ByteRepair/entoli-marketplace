# Marketing Launch Plan

A skill that plans a product or feature launch with the standards of a
product marketing manager and a launch manager, in one pass: from the
positioning line to who does what on launch day.

## What you get

- One positioning line with proof points, and the audiences it serves.
- An assets list with owners and due dates, and a launch-day sequence
  with a check before each step.
- Enablement for sales, support and success, and the measures to read
  after launch.

## Configuring

Say what is launching, who it is for, the launch date and who can own
work. The launch tier, competitor notes and business targets sharpen the
plan. It never announces or publishes anything.
