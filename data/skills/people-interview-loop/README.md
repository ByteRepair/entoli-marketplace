# People Interview Loop

A skill that designs a structured interview loop with the standards of a
recruiter and a hiring manager, in one pass. The people on the panel
interview and decide; the skill makes their materials.

## What you get

- A loop map where each competency is asked by exactly one interviewer,
  inside your rounds and time.
- A one-page kit per interviewer: behavioral questions with planned
  follow-ups and the shared off-limits list.
- A scorecard anchored at 1, 3 and 5 in observable behavior, and a
  debrief agenda that takes evidence before votes.

## Configuring

Give the role (a job description or bullets) with must-haves and
nice-to-haves, and the interviewers with the number of rounds and the
time each gets. Your team's competencies make the loop sharper.
