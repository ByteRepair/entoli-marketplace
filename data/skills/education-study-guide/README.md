# Education Study Guide

A skill that builds a personal study plan with the standards of a study
skills tutor and a subject tutor, in one pass.

## What you get

- A day-by-day schedule to the exam with spaced review, within the hours
  you have.
- Your likely weak topics first, from your own ratings, and the gaps in
  your notes.
- Practice questions for each topic, with answers drawn from your notes.

## Configuring

Give your syllabus, your notes and the exam date. A 1 to 5 confidence
rating per topic, past papers and your free hours make the plan fit you.
