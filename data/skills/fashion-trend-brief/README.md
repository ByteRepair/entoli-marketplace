# Fashion Trend Brief

A skill that opens a fashion season with the standards of a trend
researcher and a design director, in one pass: the concept brief the
rest of the label works from, built from your brand DNA first and your
pasted coverage second.

## What you get

- A theme narrative under 200 words in the brand's voice, with outside
  claims labeled "from coverage" or "assumption".
- A named palette and fabric and silhouette directions a designer can
  brief from.
- Carryover guidance and a competitor-scan checklist to fill in.

## Configuring

Give the season, brand DNA and voice, and the target customer with the
price tier. Paste runway notes or competitor pages to synthesize, and
say the cadence if it is resort or pre-fall rather than mainline.
