# Education Student Feedback

A skill that drafts feedback on student work with the standards of a
teacher and a second marker, in one pass.

## What you get

- Strengths and the one or two changes that matter most, each tied to a
  passage in the work.
- A next step the student can act on for each change.
- A suggested mark band from your rubric, kept for you to confirm and
  never stated as the final grade.

## Configuring

Give the student's work, the task and the rubric. Earlier feedback, the
tone you want and a word limit make the draft closer to how you write.
