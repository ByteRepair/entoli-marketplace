# Business Renewal Prep

A skill that prepares a customer renewal with the standards of a customer
success manager, a product manager and an account executive, in one pass.

## What you get

- The call (renew as is, renew with offer, or at risk) and the notice
  deadline.
- Value against each goal, adoption risk, and the customer's asks sorted
  against the roadmap.
- Commercial risk, a proposal inside your discount authority with a
  walk-away line, and dated next steps.

## Configuring

Paste the contract, the goals the customer bought for, usage data and
meeting notes. Support tickets, the roadmap and your discount authority
make the memo sharper.
