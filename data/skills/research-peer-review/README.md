# Research Peer Review

A skill that drafts a referee report on a submission you were asked to
review, in one pass, in the venue's form. The recommendation stays yours.

## What you get

- A summary the authors would agree with, specific strengths, and
  weaknesses ranked major then minor, each with what would fix it.
- Questions for the authors whose answers could change the assessment.
- The case for each recommendation, or support for the one you state,
  with the scores left for you to fill.

## Configuring

Paste the submission and name the venue; the review form and your notes
make the report yours. It never submits the report, never asks for your
identity, and never sends the submission text to an outside service.
