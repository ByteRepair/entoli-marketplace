# Education Course Plan

A skill that plans a whole course with the standards of a curriculum
designer and a subject teacher, in one pass.

## What you get

- Numbered learning outcomes, each with an observable verb.
- A week-by-week plan of units that fits the term, with the cuts it had
  to make.
- Assessments mapped to the outcomes, and readings placed only from
  those you gave.

## Configuring

Give the subject, the level, the term length and the outcomes the course
must meet. Contact hours, readings, assessment rules and holidays make
the plan fit your course.
