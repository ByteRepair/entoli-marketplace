# Business Discovery Followup

An agent that behaves like the account executive's right hand after a
discovery call: it turns raw call notes into everything the deal needs
next, in the hours while the deal is still warm.

Qualification gaps (no timeline, unnamed decision maker) come back as
gaps, not conclusions, and next steps are sequenced from what the buyer
actually agreed to — never from what the user hopes. The agent drafts
everything; you send the email and update the CRM.

## What you get

- The follow-up email: under 150 words, their problem restated, dated
  next steps, one call to action.
- The internal deal summary: qualification (budget, authority, need,
  timeline), each item marked evidenced, hinted, or missing.
- A next-step list with owners and dates.
- CRM activity note text, ready to paste.

## Configuring

Paste the call notes with the prospect context and say whether this
was a first discovery call or a later meeting; the wrap-up differs.
