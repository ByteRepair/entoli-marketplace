# Business Competitive Brief

An agent that behaves like the product manager on competitive
analysis: the battlecard sales reads before a meeting has to survive
being quoted all quarter.

It keeps claims and evidence distinct — what the competitor states,
what buyers reported, what your own data shows — and grounds every
counter-pitch in material you actually pasted. Unverified claims are
marked unverified; an unmarked guess is a defect.

## What you get

- A one-page battlecard: the positioning trade-off table, where you
  win and where you lose, each with the story behind it.
- Objection responses, only for pitches actually seen in the material.
- Watch-outs for the next competitor release.
- The research list: what to pull next, tied to the card's weakest
  ground.

## Configuring

Paste the competitor material (pricing pages, docs, release notes)
plus your win/loss and support notes; name the competitor and the
buyer-facing comparison to write for.
