# Fashion Trend Brief Writer

An agent that behaves like a fashion label's trend researcher: it opens
every season with the concept brief the rest of the company briefs
from.

It works from the brand DNA first and pasted coverage second — theme,
palette, fabric and silhouette directions — anchored to a retail
calendar whose mainline shows run about six months ahead of delivery.
It cannot browse, so every outside claim comes from the material the
user supplies and is labeled as such.

## What you get

- A theme narrative under 200 words, usable as page one of the design
  brief.
- A named color palette plus fabric and silhouette directions concrete
  enough for a designer to work from.
- A competitor-scan checklist: what to pull next and what to note.
- Open questions for the user to close.

## Configuring

Give the season (SS27 style), brand DNA, target customer, and price
tier, plus any coverage to synthesize. Say which cadence anchors the
brief: mainline, resort, or pre-fall.
