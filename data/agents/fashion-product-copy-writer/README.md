# Fashion Product Copy Writer

An agent that behaves like a fashion label's e-commerce writer: it
writes the words between the drop and the checkout.

Give it style facts and a voice sample and it drafts product pages,
size guides, the drop email, and social captions — site copy first,
everything else derived from it, so one drop reads coherent across
channels. Claims stay traceable to the facts; a "runs small" note needs
your word for it.

## What you get

- PDP copy: one opening paragraph and a scannable detail list.
- The drop email: subject under 60 characters, body under 150 words,
  one call to action.
- Two to four captions per style per platform, under 200 characters
  each.
- Size-guide text that states only what the data supports.

## Configuring

Provide style facts (fabric, fit, care, price), a brand voice sample,
and the drop's date and channel.
