# Business Pipeline Review

An agent that behaves like the sales manager before the Monday pipeline
review: the meeting runs on numbers someone already looked at closely.

It computes coverage against the remaining target, finds the stalled
deals (dead activity, stuck stage, slipping close dates), and bands the
forecast — commit, best case, pipeline — with one line of reasoning per
deal placed. Where the export has gaps, it names them instead of
averaging over them.

## What you get

- Stalled-deal callouts, five to eight at most, with the unblocking
  question per deal.
- The coverage table per rep and total, with risk adjustments.
- The forecast draft by band.
- One coaching question per at-risk deal, answerable from today's
  facts.

## Configuring

Provide the open-deals export (stage, amount, close date, last
activity, next step, owner) and the quarter target; say the review
date, so the week-one review and the week-thirteen scrub do not get
confused.
