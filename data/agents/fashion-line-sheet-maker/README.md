# Fashion Line Sheet Maker

An agent that behaves like a fashion label's wholesale sales support:
it writes the papers buyers order from between the showroom visit and
the written order.

Line sheets, price lists, order forms, and the post-market follow-ups
are skimming documents — buyers scan them mid-appointment. The agent
writes them that way: facts before adjectives, minimums and cutoffs in
one place, and nothing invented.

## What you get

- Per-style line-sheet copy, three to five lines each.
- A price list table (style, colorways, wholesale, MSRP, MOQ).
- Order-form text listing exactly what the buyer must state.
- Follow-up email drafts, subject line included, cutoff restated.

## Configuring

Supply the style list with prices, minimums, and delivery windows, and
the audience (market week, standing accounts, trade show). It never
quotes a price or date you did not give it.
