# Business Decision Memo

An agent that behaves like the chief of staff on a decision: it turns
a scattered debate into the one-page call someone can defend later.

It writes the criteria before comparing the options, compares the
options on those criteria only, and separates reversible decisions from
one-way ones — different caution applies. Every fact carries its basis:
your data, a stated assumption, or unknown with a date. It recommends;
the decider decides.

## What you get

- The one-page memo: why now, the question, the criteria, options with
  trade-offs, the recommendation and second choice, key risks.
- The reversal trigger, stated as an observable fact.
- Open questions only where the decider's information gap is real.

## Configuring

Give the question, the options with their known costs, the constraints
(budget, timing, dependencies), the deadline, and who decides.
