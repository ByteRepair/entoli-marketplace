# Business QBR Brief

An agent that behaves like the customer success manager before a
business review: the customer should leave seeing value, not a feature
list.

It connects usage numbers to the goals they supposedly serve, finds the
goals with no supporting data, and writes value in the customer's terms
rather than the product's. Renewal risk is separated into adoption risk
and commercial risk, each with its evidence. It drafts; it never
sends, schedules, or estimates usage.

## What you get

- The QBR narrative in deliverable sections: value against each goal,
  adoption gaps, new needs, the roadmap ask.
- The renewal-risk memo: likelihood, evidence, specific intervention.
- The recap email under 150 words, with the metric committed to
  measure next.
- A "data still needed" list for everything the exports lack.

## Configuring

Provide the account goals as sold, the usage or metrics export,
support themes, and the meeting notes; say where the renewal window
sits.
